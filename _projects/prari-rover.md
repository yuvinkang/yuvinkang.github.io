I joined Tony and Hunter to co-found Prari at Yale in 2026, leading **biology and plant science**. My work in the Ng Lab at UCD (biolistic engineering) and field work on soil nitrogen fixation shaped what the rover needs to measure, and why it matters to growers.

- **Designed** the rover and its swappable modules in SolidWorks.
- **Defined the science** behind the measurements: nitrate, pH, labile carbon, phosphorus, potassium.
- **Took part in customer discovery**: 22 farmer conversations across the U.S. and Ireland.
