I co-founded Prari at Yale in 2026 with my friends Tony Gao and Hunter Yang, leading **biology and plant science**, and I designed the rover in SolidWorks.

- **Designed** the rover body and its swappable module system in SolidWorks.
- **Defined the science** behind what it measures: nitrate, pH, labile carbon, phosphorus, potassium — drawing on my biolistics work in the Ng Lab and soil-nitrogen field work at UCD.
- **Took part in customer discovery**: 22 farmer conversations across the U.S. and Ireland that decided which sensors made the cut.
