A personal project, still in development and not yet deployed to Yale students.

- **Mapped** where Yale students travel each break, with ratings, stays and anonymized reviews for 76 places.
- **Built** friend plans by NetID; anyone not added appears only as a count.
- **Built** a flight finder that prices trips door to door from campus through Tweed, Bradley, JFK, LaGuardia, Newark or Logan, including the ride to the airport.
- **Added** sorting by cheapest, fastest and lowest-carbon, ride-cost splitting with friends, booking-time advice and an Avelo boycott switch.
- **Encoded** Yale's travel rules: State Department Level 4 countries are flagged and excluded from flight search.

Student counts and fares are demo data; booking hands off to Google Flights.
