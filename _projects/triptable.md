### Features

- **Travel map:** where Yale students go each break (spring, summer, fall, winter, weekends), shown as anonymized counts.
- **Place pages:** ratings for overall, affordability, safety and beauty, plus stays and reviews with names removed.
- **Catalog and filters:** sortable table of destinations; filter by rating, trip type (beach, mountains, city, island, desert, food) and continent.
- **Friends by NetID:** friends' plans and overlapping dates; anyone not added appears only as a count.
- **Yale travel rules:** State Department Level 4 countries are flagged and excluded from flight search.
- **Flight finder:** round-trip price per person from campus through Tweed, Bradley, JFK, LaGuardia, Newark or Logan, including the ride to the airport. Trains and buses for nearby trips.
- **Sorting:** best, cheapest, fastest or lowest carbon.
- **Avelo boycott:** one switch hides every Avelo flight.
- **Group travel:** ride costs split across friends on the trip.
- **Booking advice:** book now or wait, cheaper nearby dates, and the cheapest airport.
- **Itinerary:** saved trips and flights in one place; booking opens Google Flights.

Student counts, reviews and fares are demo data.
