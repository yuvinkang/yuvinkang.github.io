## Overview

Biolistic transformation fires DNA-coated gold microcarriers into plant cells. In the Ng Lab at University College Dublin, I worked on making that delivery more efficient in onion (*Allium cepa*) epidermis and *Nicotiana benthamiana* callus.

## What I did

- **Optimized** gold microcarrier size, helium burst pressure, and target distance.
- **Prepared** plasmid DNA and coated microcarriers.
- **Screened** transient GFP/GUS reporter expression to quantify efficiency.

## Result

- **43% increase** in transformation efficiency.
