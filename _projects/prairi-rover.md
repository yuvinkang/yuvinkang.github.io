## Overview

Prairi Robotics is building an autonomous rover for precision farming. It drives crop rows and collects soil and plant health data at meter resolution, so farmers can act on problems plant by plant instead of field by field.

## What I did

- **Co-founded** the venture and defined the rover's sensing goals.
- **Designed** the CAD model and first prototype in SolidWorks.
- **Worked with 20+ farmers** to shape the design direction.

## Where it's going

We're working to fund paid pilots in Dublin and North Carolina.
