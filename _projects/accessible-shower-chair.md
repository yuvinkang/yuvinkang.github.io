I designed the chair at Product Quickstart, from the first sketches through the SolidWorks model and 3D-printed prototypes.

- **Modelled** the full assembly in SolidWorks: frame, carriage, seat, leg lift and the cable drive.
- **Sized** the frame around a standard tub wall so the outboard legs stand on the floor and the inboard pair stands in the tub.
- **Designed** the drive as one gearmotor and a closed cable loop, so the same motor carries the seat in and back out.
- **Printed** and iterated the lock, the leg-support socket and the foot cups until they assembled without tools.
